# HW3 Sources

Access date for every document: 2026-09-21

## california_tenants_2026.pdf

- Title: California Tenants: A Guide to Residential Tenants' and Landlords' Rights and Responsibilities, 2026 Edition
- Organization: California Department of Real Estate
- URL: https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/ResourceGuidebook/2026_Landlord_Tenant_Guide.pdf
- Why it is relevant: It explains California rules for residential rentals, including security deposits and application screening fees.

## hud_rental_screening_guidance.pdf

- Title: Guidance on Application of the Fair Housing Act to the Screening of Applicants for Rental Housing
- Organization: U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity
- URL: https://archives.hud.gov/news/2024/FHEO_Guidance_on_Screening_of_Applicants_for_Rental_Housing.pdf
- Why it is relevant: It explains how Fair Housing Act rules apply when landlords and screening companies review rental applicants.

## cfpb_tenant_background_market_report.pdf

- Title: Tenant Background Checks Market Report
- Organization: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
- URL: https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_tenant-background-checks-market_report_2022-11.pdf
- Why it is relevant: It describes the tenant screening report market that landlords use when deciding whether to rent.

## cfpb_tenant_background_consumer_snapshot.pdf

- Title: Consumer Snapshot: Tenant Background Checks
- Organization: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
- URL: https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_consumer-snapshot-tenant-background-check_2022-11.pdf
- Why it is relevant: It summarizes renter complaints about errors and other problems in tenant screening reports.

## ftc_rental_listing_scams.html

- Title: Rental Listing Scams
- Organization: Federal Trade Commission
- URL: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/rental-listing-scams
- Why it is relevant: It explains how fake rental listings work and how renters can avoid paying a scammer.

## ftc_rental_scams_data_spotlight.html
